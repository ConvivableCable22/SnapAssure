Meet SOMA — Your Event Concierge

SOMA is SnapAssure's dedicated AI assistant, crafted to help you discover, plan, and book unforgettable interactive photo and video experiences for your special celebrations.

What SOMA Can Do For You

Your 24/7 personal guide for events & celebrations:

  • Curated Recommendations: Tell SOMA your event type — wedding, corporate summit, brand activation, birthday, or college fest — and get tailored booth suggestions for your vibe and guest count.
  • Explore 44+ Experiences: Learn about 360 Spinners, Glam Robot Cams, Vintage Mirror Booths, AI Morphing, Slow-Mo Tunnels, and more.
  • Inclusions & Space Requirements: Get instant answers on venue dimensions, backdrop options, custom branding, instant printouts, and digital sharing.
  • Catalogue & Site Navigation: SOMA can navigate the website with you, filter booths by category, and highlight live client showcases.
  • Quick Enquiry Assistance: Ready for a quote? SOMA gathers your key event details so our production team can send you a personalized proposal right away.
  • Creative Brainstorming & Everyday Chat: Need photo booth theme concepts, wedding hashtag ideas, or answers to general questions? SOMA is always ready to talk.

How to Interact

  • Type or Speak: Chat by typing or tap the microphone icon anytime to talk hands-free.
  • Natural Questions: Ask naturally, such as "What booth is best for an outdoor cocktail party?" or "Show me experiences with instant prints."
