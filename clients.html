    Meet SOMA — Your Event Concierge

    Your 24/7 personal guide for events & celebrations:

    • Curated Recommendations: Tell SOMA your event type — wedding, corporate summit, brand activation, birthday, or college fest — and get tailored booth suggestions for your vibe and guest count.
    • Explore 44+ Experiences: Learn about 360 Spinners, Glam Robot Cams, Vintage Mirror Booths, AI Morphing, Slow-Mo Tunnels, and more.
